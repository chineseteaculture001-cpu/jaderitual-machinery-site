Questions buyers ask

Before you enquire

Are you a manufacturer?

No. We are a procurement and export partner. We buy across our supplier network, consolidate the order and ship it — and we say so plainly so buyers know who they are dealing with.

Can I start with a small order?

Yes. Small parts can be supplied as a sample or trial quantity by courier before you commit to a container.

Can different categories go in one container?

Yes, and that is usually the point. Undercarriage, hydraulic parts, pump wear parts, delivery line and welding consumables can be combined into one loading plan.

How do I get a price?

Send the part number, a photo of the old part, or the machine model and the quantity. Parts are matched by number, not by description, so those details set the price.
