Privacy Policy

JadeRitual Machinery ("we", "us", "our") operates the website machinery.thejaderitual.com (the "Site") as the machinery and parts division of JadeRitual Group.

What we collect

When you submit an enquiry we collect the details you provide: company name, contact name, email address, destination country, the category and quantities you are asking about, and any part numbers or machine details you send. We also collect standard server log data.

Why we collect it

To answer your enquiry, prepare a quotation, arrange shipment and keep a record of the commercial relationship.

Sharing

We share the minimum necessary detail with the suppliers and forwarders involved in fulfilling your order. We do not sell your data.

Translation tool

This Site offers an optional machine-translation widget. When you use it, the page text is sent to the translation provider for processing. Using the widget is your choice; the English version is the authoritative one.

Cookies

The Site itself does not set marketing cookies. Third-party tools such as the translation widget may set their own cookies.

Your rights

Write to us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of the information you have sent us.
